{# ---- Who / where / how ---- #}
How expert rating works
Who rates?
  • Admin — overall quality oversight
  • Instructor / domain expert — subject accuracy & pedagogy
  • Learners — SUS survey only (not this page)
Where?
  • This page: /admin/expert_ratings
  • Sidebar → Expert Ratings
  • Open course in admin panel → review materials → rate once
What is rated?
  • Completeness, Relevance
  • Hallucination risk (inverted stars)
  • Learning gain & expert usability
  • Not generation time (automatic only)
